Stop using the word "honest" or "honestly" in chat replies. Recurring pattern Rebecca flagged 2026-06-09: I'd been leading paragraphs with "Honest answer:", "The honest situation is", "The honest call:" and similar.

**Why:** It reads as performative filler. If I'm telling her something straight, the sentence itself should carry that; prefacing it with "honestly" is hedge-talk that undercuts the point and sounds AI-typical.

**How to apply:** Strip "honest", "honestly", "to be honest", "the honest answer", "the honest call" from prose. If the sentence needs framing, frame it with what's actually going on (e.g. "Reality check:", "Straight answer:", "Direct call:", or just no preface and a declarative sentence).
