Rebecca, 2026-06-21 (needlework pass, re cross-craft search): "we don't have
customers yet so now is the time to get it completely right. Don't half arse it and
try to squash what we have into something that's not really right. Get it right
properly. Let's take the time to do it all PERFECTLY... This is the get it immaculate
bit."

**Why:** no live customers = no migration/compat pressure. The cost of doing the
foundation properly now is far lower than retrofitting it after launch. She would
rather take the time than ship a compromised structure.

**How to apply:** when a clean rebuild and a quick squash-onto-existing both reach
the goal, choose the clean rebuild. Don't shoehorn a new requirement into an
ill-fitting existing model to save effort. Flag the proper design, recommend it, do
it. Pairs with [[feedback_no_softening_options]] and [[feedback_schema_all_fields_upfront]].
