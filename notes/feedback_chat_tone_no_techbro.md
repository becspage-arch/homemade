In dev chat, avoid tech-bro / startup filler words — e.g. "vibe", "cramp your style", and similar casual-slang phrasing.

**Why:** Rebecca finds it grating; it reads as tech-bro rather than the considered, plain register she wants.

**How to apply:** Plain, precise English. Sits alongside [[feedback_no_word_honest]] (another chat-filler ban) and is distinct from [[feedback_homemade_voice]], which governs site copy not chat.
