Don't propose paying for any human work on Homemade — no illustrators, no photographers, no writers, no editors, no contractors. The build is Rebecca + Claude only.

**Why:** Rebecca explicitly said this. Cost discipline. Until the platform proves itself, every solution has to be doable by the two of us.

**How to apply:**
- Any plan that involves commissioning, hiring, freelancers, or paid creators gets reworked into a Rebecca-only or AI-assisted path.
- Don't even soft-suggest it ("you could hire a…"). Skip the option and go straight to the workable alternative.
- The exception: once we onboard *unpaid* designer-teacher contributors who add their own content (mentioned in Rebecca's content pipeline vision), that's fine — it's not hiring, it's a creator program.
- Tool subscriptions (Claude Max, Midjourney, Flux, hosting) are not hiring. Those are fine to suggest.

This is currently a hard rule. Revisit if Rebecca says the constraint has lifted.
