When working with Mindset tutorials (PRACTICE / READING type), any text that comes verbatim from Rebecca's books must stay exactly as written. This includes:

- Affirmations
- Energy alignment statements
- Energy release statements
- Specific phrasings like "I am ready to", "release feeling stuck", "align/allow", "I claim", "I release", "I receive"
- Tapping scripts where the exact wording matters to the practice
- Any quoted phrase she has authored elsewhere

**Why:** Rebecca said (2026-05-25 after the voice pilot): "do not change any energy alignment & release statements, EG anything that's already taken verbatim from my books should remain as is." These are her IP and the wording is intentional.

**How to apply:**
- Worker prompts for mindset retrofitting MUST include this rule.
- Voice register / grade-6-8 rewrites do NOT apply to these statements. They are exempt from sentence-shortening, vocabulary-simplifying, or rephrasing.
- Surrounding prose (orientation paragraphs, "how this practice works" intros, methodology framing) CAN be rewritten to register. The statements themselves cannot.
- If unsure whether a piece of mindset text is verbatim from a book vs. authored fresh for homemade.education, default to NOT rewriting. Leave it alone and flag to Rebecca.
- The Mindset voice memory (feedback_mindset_voice.md) still applies to non-verbatim mindset prose — factual, recipe-clean register for surrounding text. Verbatim statements are an exception, not the rule.
