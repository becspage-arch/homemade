Rebecca explicitly said: no time estimates, ever. Not for my work ("this will take a session"), not for hers ("~30 minutes to sign up"), not for anything. There is no timeframe for Homemade. The work takes as long as it takes.

**How to apply:**
- Cut all "this will take ~X" / "in roughly Y" / "by tomorrow" phrasing
- Cut "quickly", "soon", "shortly" used as duration estimates
- Cut signup time estimates ("~30 min total")
- Cut session-size estimates ("one focused session", "by end of today")
- Just describe what will happen, in what order, without estimating duration

**Why:** It's noise. It sets up false expectations. It implies pressure that doesn't exist.

**Where it's fine:**
- Concrete technical timeouts: "the deploy takes 30 seconds" if that's a real measured fact about the system
- Cron schedules: "runs every 5 minutes"
- Actual deadlines if Rebecca sets one explicitly

The line: don't estimate human work duration. State factual machine durations only when they matter for the task.
