# Crochet quality bar (Rebecca, 9 October 2026)

**The rule:** a crochet piece passes only when it's customer-ready. That means it would sit next to the best-selling crochet patterns on Etsy and look as beautiful and cute as they do. "Acceptable, could be better" is a tweak, never a pass. Rebecca: "If it's not customer-ready, it does not pass yet."

Rebecca's five example listings (kept outside the repo, in the project's shared folder `/mnt/project-files/homemade/crochet-bar/`, because they are other shops' images and this repo is public) are examples of the level of beauty and cuteness, not a complete list. More examples from top-selling patterns are in `research-top-patterns.md`, and `real/` holds 47 checked real photos from top Ravelry patterns sorted by engine round (`real/INDEX.md`), all in that same shared folder. The element-by-element audit of the engine is the Claude Doc "Crochet engine audit against the bar".

## What customer-ready means

1. **It is instantly loveable.** At thumbnail size you know what it is and you want it. Cute comes from proportion: a big head, a small soft body, chunky rounded limbs, eyes set low and wide, and a small muzzle.
2. **The yarn looks real and soft.** Chenille reads as velvety plush with no visible ply. Cotton is crisp, with small, even stitches and clear definition. Yarn never looks like rope, plastic or a twisted cable.
3. **It is posed like a toy, not a diagram.** It sits or stands naturally. Arms rest forward or fold on the tummy, and legs point forward when seated. It has weight and settles slightly into the surface. Nothing sticks out stiffly.
4. **It has a face with character.** Safety eyes placed with intent, or embroidered features: sleeping eyes, lashes, a stitched nose and mouth, and blush on the cheeks. The face is symmetrical and gentle.
5. **The signature details are there.** These are the touches that make a listing sell: lined ears in a contrast colour, toe-bean paw pads, a fringe or curls, a hat with a pompom, tiny flowers, ruffled edges, wings, a small accessory.
6. **The colour is a designed palette.** Soft, harmonious colours (creams, dusty pinks, sage, lilac, warm browns, or Christmas green and gold) with one or two accents. No harsh primaries unless the design calls for them.
7. **The construction is honest.** Every part is made the way a real maker would crochet it, and the written pattern produces exactly what is shown.
8. **It is photographed like a bestseller.** Warm natural light, a soft styled background (linen, a windowsill, a nursery shelf, a Christmas branch), shallow depth of field, and the piece sharp and centred. The listing photo is a styled scene, not a render on grey.

## How to judge

- Put the render beside at least one bar photo at the same size and ask: would a customer choose ours? If not, it fails, and the reason is written down.
- Judge the base render for construction and the finished hero for beauty. Both must pass.
- Rebecca signs off. A Claude verdict is a draft until she agrees.
